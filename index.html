Rewards you already earn shouldn't need a memory test.

Every card has a different rate, cap, and exclusion — and no one keeps that straight at the register. Most tools track spend after the fact. Almost none tell you if you used the right instrument.

Six cards, one memory

Nobody holds every reward rate, cap, and exclusion in their head at checkout. So the default card wins, not the best one.

Tracking without judgment

Passive spend trackers show what you spent. None of them tell you whether you spent it on the right card.

Points that just sit there

Miles and points expire quietly, or get spent on the first thing you see instead of where they're worth the most.

No plan for the big purchase

A laptop, a trip, an EMI — the moment it matters most, there's no guidance on which card actually pays for it.

How it works

Three habits, not a new app to babysit.

01

Plan

Pick a category and an amount — get the best card from the ones you actually hold, ranked by what it'll actually earn.

02

Log

Note what you actually used, in a few taps. EMI purchases split into the right month automatically.

03

Audit

See the gap between what you earned and what your best card would've earned — every month, in plain rupees.

04

Redeem

A dated reference for what your points and miles are actually worth, and where to spend them.

What's in it

Dashboard

Spend, earnings, and what you missed — one screen, updated as you log.

Future purchase planning

Add a trip or a big purchase ahead of time and see which card fits — including whether it'll push you past a fee waiver or milestone tier.

Monthly budget limit

Set a number, watch the bar fill as the month goes, get a clear amber-then-red warning before you're over.

Redemption guide

Fee, forex, transfer partners, and a visible "last verified" date on every card you hold.

Card requests

Don't see your card yet? Request it and upvote existing requests — tells us exactly what to curate next.
