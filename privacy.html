This is a plain-language starting draft, not reviewed by a lawyer. Have it checked before treating it as your actual, binding policy — especially once real accounts, email collection, or payment handling are added.

What Cardpare stores today

In its current form, Cardpare does not have user accounts and does not run its own server-side database. The cards you select, the purchases you log, and any budget limit you set are stored directly in your own browser (or, if you're using Cardpare inside Claude, synced privately to your Claude account). We do not receive or see this data ourselves.

Card requests and the waitlist

If you submit a card request or join the early-access waitlist, that information is collected so we can improve the card database and contact you about access. Depending on how Cardpare is deployed, this may be visible to other users of a shared prototype, or held in a simple list we review manually — we'll update this section with specifics once a real backend is in place.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate website analytics (such as page views and general location by country) to understand usage. This does not identify you individually. If we ever add more detailed analytics or advertising trackers, this policy will be updated first.

Affiliate links

Some card recommendations may include a referral link to the issuing bank or a partner network. If you apply for a card through such a link, the bank or network may receive a referral fee, and their own privacy policy governs whatever information you provide during that application — we do not receive your application details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to third parties.
  • We don't read your actual bank or card statements unless you've explicitly connected that feature in a future version, with your clear consent at that time.
  • We don't share individually identifiable spend data with card issuers or advertisers.

Your choices

You can clear your locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for Cardpare, or by using any in-app reset option if provided. You can ask us to remove a submitted card request or waitlist entry by contacting us.
